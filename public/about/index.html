About me

I'm Brian — an Electrical & Electronic Engineer by training, and a software engineer in practice. I build for the web, mostly the parts users never see: the APIs, the data models, the background jobs, and the quiet infrastructure that keeps everything upright.

Lately I've been drawn further down the stack. I've become quite fond of bytes and the internals of software... how things actually work underneath the abstractions we reach for every day. Most of my work lives in Python 🐍 and Rust 🦀, usually somewhere near a PostgreSQL database.

I'm a strong believer in open source and in learning in public, which is a large part of why this blog exists at all.

About this blog

I have procastinated on owning and managing a personal blog for more than 3 forevers, the major hestiation I think was largely that i found it very stressful and unfun to write articles and worry about building the html to structure said article.

Secondly, I have tried using a blogging platform like Medium, it was fun for a very short while but it was not too long before i found that it became an unfun chore too, I found myself trying to maintain a certain writing style I perceived to be perform better, ensuring that my articles reached a certain amount of words even when the idea and message behind them were truly shared, and doing all manners of shenanigans with SEO to ensure that my articles even got seen by readers.

This was not the idea I had for writing, I want to write and sound the way I feel when I am writing. It could be a single paragraph rant on some very niche topic, or an ungodly devotion-style deep dive into a topic. I want to do all of these and have full control of my structure, content and not worrying about trying to keep a reader who might be distracted by another buzz keyword SEO Optimized article showing in the sidebar. Also if a reader do really love my work, I am not opposed to getting support in form of sharing my work or tipping me (You should definitely tip me sometime).

So one day, I wrote down the idea I wanted for my blog.

The site itself is built with Zola, a static site generator that compiles Markdown into plain HTML, with no JavaScript framework anywhere near it.

If something here helped, or if I got something wrong, I'd genuinely like to hear about it.
